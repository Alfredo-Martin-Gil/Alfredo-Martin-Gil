### Three connected projects

**Clinical NLP Triage — applied research prototype**  
An auditable rules-based prototype for lexical signal detection in free-text triage notes. It uses 180 synthetic cases, publishes negative results and error analysis, includes nine software tests, and runs synthetic evaluation in CI. Zero detected lexicon signals are explicitly not interpreted as low clinical risk.  
[Repository](https://github.com/Alfredo-Martin-Gil/clinical-nlp-triage-open-source) · [Current integration commit](https://github.com/Alfredo-Martin-Gil/clinical-nlp-triage-open-source/commit/8381a93896b1175ffbe1a85b2fa82d6454396d4d)

**Clinical Reflection Band — research concept**  
A conceptual architecture exploring whether a minimal, non-directive cue could support reassessment when a clinician's working frame becomes less coherent with an evolving patient trajectory.  
[Repository](https://github.com/Alfredo-Martin-Gil/clinical-reflection-band) · [Consolidation commit](https://github.com/Alfredo-Martin-Gil/clinical-reflection-band/commit/8611ecdc95cd6f3dda02694f35a4f237609f57ed)

**Prehospital Clinical Decision Uncertainty — clinical foundation**  
A clinical-operational analysis of incomplete information, evolving patient state, reassessment, handover, and responsibility boundaries before technology is introduced.  
[Repository](https://github.com/Alfredo-Martin-Gil/prehospital-clinical-decision-uncertainty) · [Consolidation commit](https://github.com/Alfredo-Martin-Gil/prehospital-clinical-decision-uncertainty/commit/73075aa0d1f24b4a3e541b1fba1ebe02e7b7fe60)

### What this portfolio demonstrates

- clinical workflow and implementation reasoning;
- transparent synthetic evaluation and error analysis;
- attention to false negatives and false reassurance;
- traceability and documentation–implementation reconciliation;
- human-oversight and governance boundaries;
- communication across clinical, technical, research, and product teams.

### Evidence boundary

The portfolio contains a synthetic-data research prototype, a conceptual architecture, and a clinical-operational analysis. It does not demonstrate clinical validation, patient use, hospital deployment, improved outcomes, medical-device readiness, Health Canada compliance, or an active/certified QMS. No real patient data are used.
