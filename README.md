# Alfredo Martín Gil, MD

**Clinical AI & Healthcare Implementation Physician**

Physician working at the intersection of clinical workflows, auditable AI prototypes, patient-safety boundaries, human oversight, evaluation, and healthcare implementation.

My public portfolio is intentionally small: one applied research prototype, one conceptual safety architecture, and one clinical-operational foundation. Together, they show how I move from a real workflow problem to a bounded technical exploration and a research agenda.

## Selected Clinical AI portfolio

### 1. Clinical NLP Triage — applied research prototype

[**clinical-nlp-triage-open-source**](https://github.com/Alfredo-Martin-Gil/clinical-nlp-triage-open-source)

An open-source research prototype for auditable clinical risk-signal detection in free-text triage notes. It uses synthetic data and transparent rule-based logic to make assumptions, failure modes, and evaluation boundaries inspectable.

**Demonstrates:** clinical problem framing, traceability, synthetic evaluation, conservative failure messaging, human oversight, and governance documentation.

**Boundary:** research and education only. It is not clinically validated, deployed, production-ready, or intended for real-patient decisions.

### 2. Clinical Reflection Band — research concept

[**clinical-reflection-band**](https://github.com/Alfredo-Martin-Gil/clinical-reflection-band)

A research-stage conceptual architecture exploring whether AI could support reassessment when the clinician's working frame becomes less coherent with an evolving patient trajectory.

**Demonstrates:** safety-oriented research framing, human factors, workflow boundaries, failure-mode thinking, and validation planning.

**Boundary:** conceptual research artifact. It has no implemented algorithm, clinical validation, deployment, or regulatory status.

### 3. Prehospital Clinical Decision Uncertainty — clinical foundation

[**prehospital-clinical-decision-uncertainty**](https://github.com/Alfredo-Martin-Gil/prehospital-clinical-decision-uncertainty)

A clinical-operational analysis of decision-making under uncertainty in prehospital care, using acute dyspnea as a worked context.

**Demonstrates:** workflow analysis, uncertainty management, reassessment, responsibility boundaries, and requirements for responsible decision support.

**Boundary:** operational analysis, not a guideline, protocol, algorithm, or software implementation.

## How the three projects connect

1. **Clinical foundation:** describe the workflow, uncertainty, and responsibility boundaries.
2. **Applied prototype:** test a transparent, auditable approach with synthetic data and explicit failure analysis.
3. **Research concept:** explore a complementary human-factors question around reassessment and cognitive safety.

This sequence supports implementation work without turning a prototype or concept into evidence of clinical deployment.

## Working focus

- Clinical workflow and implementation analysis
- Patient safety, human oversight, and failure-mode analysis
- Evaluation and validation planning
- Explainability, traceability, and governance boundaries
- Communication across clinical, technical, research, and product teams

## Evidence and representation boundaries

The repositories above contain different kinds of evidence: a research prototype, a conceptual architecture, and a clinical-operational analysis. None should be described as:

- a validated clinical AI system;
- a deployed hospital implementation;
- proven to improve clinical outcomes;
- a production-ready medical device;
- compliant with Health Canada or another regulator;
- supported by an active or certified quality-management system.

Synthetic benchmark results are engineering evidence only and do not establish clinical performance or safety.
